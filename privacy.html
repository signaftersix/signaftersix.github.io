Privacy policy

This policy explains what information Sign After Six Mobile Notary collects, why it is used, who processes it, and when uploaded documents are deleted.

Information collected

An appointment request may include the customer's name, email address, phone number, service address, requested time, document category, service details, accessibility or facility information voluntarily provided, customer comments, payment status, and uploaded documents or clear page photographs.

How information is used

Information is used to prepare and review quotes, determine travel and availability, communicate with the customer, perform the requested service, process payment, maintain calendar holds, prevent duplicate bookings, send reminders, respond to cancellation or rescheduling requests, protect against misuse, and maintain a limited operational audit trail.

Uploaded documents

Uploaded documents are stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket and are not stored in the public GitHub website repository. Access is limited to authorized administration through expiring signed links. Documents are locked after review. They are scheduled for deletion seven days after an appointment is marked completed. After a final cancellation, documents are scheduled for deletion within 24 hours unless a temporary payment-dispute, fraud, legal, or operational hold is necessary.

Service providers

Supabase provides database, authentication, server-function, and private-file storage services. Google provides business email and calendar services. Square processes payments. Mapbox provides address matching, mileage, and travel-time routing. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

Payment information

Card details are entered directly into Square and are not stored by this website. Sign After Six stores limited payment references, status, amount, and deadlines needed to reconcile an appointment.

Security

The administrative dashboard requires an authorized Google account and a second authentication factor. Customer documents use private storage and short-lived access links. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, but access controls, restricted browser origins, server-side validation, audit logging, and limited retention are used to reduce risk.

Customer choices

A customer may contact Sign After Six to ask about their appointment information, correct inaccurate details, or request deletion when retention is no longer operationally or legally necessary. Some limited transaction or audit information may be retained when reasonably required for business, fraud-prevention, tax, dispute, or legal purposes.

Contact

Privacy questions and requests may be sent to signaftersix@gmail.com.
