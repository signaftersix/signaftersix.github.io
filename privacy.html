Privacy

Privacy notice

Draft for launch review. This page describes the intended data handling built into the repository. Review it before publishing live.

Information collected

Appointment requests may include your name, email address, phone number, service address, appointment details, document category, accessibility or facility information you choose to provide, payment status, and uploaded document files.

Document uploads

Uploaded documents are intended to be stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket, not in the public GitHub repository. Access should be restricted to authorized administration. Uploaded document files are configured for deletion seven days after a completed appointment.

Payments

Payments are handled through Square. Sign After Six should store payment status and transaction references, not full payment-card details.

Messages and scheduling

Email notices may be sent using the business Gmail account. Transactional SMS may be sent using a separate SMS provider. Approved and pending appointment information may sync to selected Google Calendars.

Analytics

The site may collect basic usage and conversion events such as page views, quote starts, completed requests, approvals, and payment conversions. Uploaded document content should never be sent to analytics.

Contact

Questions about this notice may be sent to signaftersix@gmail.com.
