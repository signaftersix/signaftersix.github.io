Quick answers

FAQ

Do you offer remote or online notarization?

No. Sign After Six provides in-person mobile notarization only.

How quickly can I get a same-day appointment?

Available same-day slots appear in the booking flow. If the day is full, you can request an emergency opening for manual review. Rush pricing depends on how much notice you provide and whether the appointment is after 8 PM.

What identification should I bring?

Common options include a current Florida driver license or ID, U.S. passport, certain other government-issued IDs, and other forms Florida law recognizes. Final acceptance depends on the circumstances and the applicable legal requirements.

Can you tell me which notarial act I need?

No. I can perform the requested act, but I can’t choose the legal act for you. If you’re unsure, check the document instructions or ask the document issuer or an attorney.

Do you provide witnesses?

No. If your document requires witnesses, you’re responsible for having them present and eligible.

Can you print my document?

Yes. Printing is $5 for up to 10 pages, then $0.25 per additional page.

Can you mail the document afterward?

Yes. I can provide a standard envelope and stamp, drop it in a mailbox, or take it to a staffed post-office counter. If mailing assistance is selected, the document will be sent within 24 hours of signing.

What happens after I request a time?

The time is held as Pending while I review your request and document. If approved, you’ll receive a payment link. The appointment is confirmed after payment.
