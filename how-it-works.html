How It Works

Requesting an appointment is simple.

1

Build your quote

Enter the service address first, then choose from the times that are actually available for that location.

2

Send the document

Upload a PDF or clear photos. You can request a time without it, but the appointment can’t be approved until it’s reviewed.

3

I review it

Your requested time is held while the document and quote are reviewed. You approve any increase before paying.

4

Pay securely

After approval, Square handles payment. Payment confirmation changes the appointment to confirmed and starts reminders.
