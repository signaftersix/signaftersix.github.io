Important information

Booking terms

Draft for launch review. These terms implement the business rules selected for this website and should be reviewed before live publication.

Pending requests

Submitting the form requests an appointment; it does not create a confirmed appointment. The requested time is held as Pending while the request and any required document are reviewed.

Quotes

The website quote is an estimate based on the information provided. If review requires a higher revised quote, the customer must accept it within the stated window before approval. Lower reviewed prices do not require new acceptance.

Payment

Payment is requested only after approval. For appointments within 24 hours, payment is due immediately after approval. For appointments more than 24 hours away, payment is due within four hours or the slot may be released.

Cancellation

24+ hours before an appointment: full refund. 4–24 hours: refund less a $10 reservation/cancellation charge. Less than four hours but before travel begins: the notarial-act charge and unused add-ons are refundable, while 50% of mobile/travel/urgency service charges may be retained. After travel begins, mobile/travel/urgency service charges are nonrefundable. If no notarization occurs, no notarial-act fee is retained. Completed printing is nonrefundable. If Sign After Six cancels, the customer receives a full refund.

Rescheduling

One reschedule is available without a rescheduling charge when requested at least 12 hours in advance. The quote is recalculated for a new time, date, address, distance, or urgency level.

Late arrival

A 10-minute grace period applies. If the appointment cannot be completed after that point, mobile/travel charges may remain nonrefundable.

Witnesses and identification

Customers are responsible for providing any required witnesses. Every signer must satisfy applicable identification and personal-appearance requirements.

No legal advice

Sign After Six Mobile Notary is not a law firm. The notary does not provide legal advice, prepare legal documents, or choose the notarial act for a customer.
