Customer policies

Booking policies

These policies explain when an appointment becomes confirmed, what customers must have ready, and how cancellations, rescheduling, and refunds are handled.

Requests and confirmation

Submitting the form creates a pending request, not a confirmed appointment. Signed After Six reviews the appointment details and documents first. The appointment becomes confirmed only after approval and completed payment.

Quotes and payment deadlines

The website estimate is recalculated from the information submitted and reviewed before approval. A higher reviewed quote must be accepted through the secure quote-review link. Once approved, appointments more than 24 hours away have a four-hour payment window. Appointments within 24 hours have a 60-minute payment window. An unpaid request may expire and its calendar hold may be released.

Cancellation

A cancellation requested at least four hours before the appointment normally receives a full refund. With less than four hours' notice, mobile, travel, urgency, holiday, completed printing, and other convenience services already committed may be nonrefundable. Charges for notarial acts or a marriage solemnization that were not performed are refunded. A cancellation request does not cancel the appointment automatically; the appointment remains scheduled until Signed After Six confirms the cancellation.

Rescheduling

One reschedule may be approved without a separate rescheduling charge when requested at least four hours beforehand. A later request, a second reschedule, or a material change in address or service details requires manual review and may change the quote. The original appointment remains scheduled until the replacement time passes availability and travel checks and is approved.

No-shows and readiness

A customer is treated as unavailable after a reasonable 15-minute wait unless another arrangement is made. Mobile, travel, and applicable convenience charges may be retained when the customer is unavailable or the appointment cannot proceed because acceptable identification, required signers, required witnesses, or a complete document are missing. No charge for an unperformed notarial act is retained.

Notarial discretion

A notarization may be refused when the signer is absent, unwilling, unaware, improperly identified, appears coerced, or when the requested act cannot lawfully be completed. If Signed After Six cancels for a reason not caused by the customer, the customer receives a full refund.

Witnesses, identification, and documents

Customers are responsible for confirming whether witnesses are required and for providing them unless another arrangement is approved in writing. Each signer must appear with acceptable identification. Documents should be complete but unsigned where the signature must occur before the notary. Signed After Six does not select the notarial act or advise a customer how to complete a document.

Parking, mailing, and outside costs

Paid parking is charged at actual cost without markup. Mailing and certified-mail charges follow the itemized quote. Tolls are not charged. Refunds are processed manually through Square at launch and may take additional time to appear according to the customer's financial institution.

Service limitations

Signed After Six provides in-person Florida mobile notary services and simple marriage solemnizations. It does not provide remote online notarization, legal advice, document preparation, attorney services, or loan-signing-agent services.

Questions may be sent to signaftersix@gmail.com.
